Professional referrals

A clear route into care at home.

For clinicians, discharge planners, case managers and care organizations exploring home-based primary care for an adult patient.

Potential referral fit

Start with access, complexity and the home context.

A referral conversation may be appropriate for an adult who has difficulty reaching an office or whose care benefits from seeing the home context.

  • Mobility or transportation barriers
  • Several chronic conditions, medications or care teams to coordinate
  • A transition home after hospitalization or rehabilitation
  • Family or caregiver coordination needs

Clinical eligibility, location, coverage, availability and the appropriate visit type are confirmed individually by the practice.

Protect patient information

Call before sending a clinical record.

This public website and ordinary email are not secure referral channels. Do not send diagnoses, medication lists, discharge summaries, insurance details or other protected health information through them.

Referral partners

Built for coordinated transitions.

The referral route is intended for professionals helping an adult move safely between settings or establish ongoing primary care at home.

  • Hospital and rehabilitation discharge teams
  • Skilled-nursing and assisted-living professionals
  • Home health and hospice teams
  • Primary and specialty clinicians
  • Social workers and case managers
