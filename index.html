Who we serve

Is home-based care worth asking about?

It may be worth a conversation when travel is difficult, care is medically complex or the home context matters to the plan.

  • Mobility or transportation makes office visits difficult
  • Several chronic conditions need ongoing coordination
  • Family or caregivers help organize care
  • A transition home needs closer clinical follow-up

This list is not a clinical eligibility decision. The practice confirms fit individually. For emergencies, call 911.

Ask a different question
Is this the same as home health?

No. Nabia’s website describes clinician-led primary care. Home health, personal care, therapy and hospice are different services. Ask which services Nabia provides and which may require another organization.

Must someone be completely homebound?

Not every person with difficulty reaching an office has the same needs. Contact the practice to confirm current eligibility and whether the requested visit type is available.

Can I contact Nabia for a loved one?

You may make a general inquiry. The practice may need the patient’s permission or other legal authority before discussing private health information with anyone else.

Does Nabia replace the current primary-care clinician?

That depends on the requested service and care arrangement. Ask whether Nabia would provide ongoing primary care, a limited service or coordination with the current clinical team.

How do we confirm insurance and cost?

Call the practice with the plan name only. Do not send a member ID through this website or ordinary email. Confirm participation and expected payment before care begins.
