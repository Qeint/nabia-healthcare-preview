Website notice

Privacy and communication

Important limits for using this public website and ordinary email.

Not for emergencies

This website is not monitored for urgent medical needs. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department for an emergency.

Do not send private medical information

The website’s consultation form opens an ordinary email message and does not store the form on the website. Do not send diagnoses, symptoms, dates of birth, insurance or member IDs, medical records, prescriptions, Social Security numbers or other sensitive information through the form or ordinary email.

No clinician-patient relationship

Using this website, sending a message or receiving general information does not create a clinician-patient relationship. Care begins only after the practice confirms acceptance and completes its intake process.

Website data

This version of the website does not intentionally use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, account registration or an online database. A hosting provider may still record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested page and time for security and operations. Hosting practices should be confirmed before launch.

Accuracy and availability

Services, appointment availability, service area, insurance participation and costs can change. Contact the office to confirm current information before relying on it.

Questions

For website or privacy questions, call 202-810-4598 or email info@nabiahealthcare.com.
