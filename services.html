Healthcare services

Primary care, wherever home is.

Home-based primary care leads the practice, with office and virtual options considered according to the person, visit and current availability.

Lead service

Home-based primary care

Medical care in a home or residential setting for people who have difficulty traveling or benefit from care in their own environment.

  • Primary care visits at home
  • Fall-risk and safety assessment
  • Post-hospital follow-up
  • Caregiver and family coordination
  • Support for mobility limitations
Ongoing care

Family practice

Primary-care visits shaped around prevention, medication review and ongoing health needs.

  • Annual wellness visits
  • Preventive screenings
  • Medication review and management
  • Health education
  • Chronic condition follow-up
Virtual option

Telemedicine

Virtual visits for eligible concerns when an in-person examination is not required.

  • Follow-up visits
  • Medication refills and reviews
  • Lab-result discussions
  • Chronic disease check-ins
  • Care planning
Connected care

Care coordination

Communication and planning for patients and families managing several moving parts.

  • Extended care planning
  • Medication coordination
  • Family and caregiver communication
  • Preventive health planning
  • Coordination with specialists
Important: Available services depend on clinical appropriateness, location, scheduling and coverage. Call the office to confirm whether a service is available for you.

Why the setting matters

The visit is one part of the care plan.

Daily routines, mobility, medication organization and caregiver support can affect what is practical after an appointment. Home-based care lets the clinician consider that context directly.
