Healthcare services

Primary care in the setting that fits.

Office appointments, home and facility visits, and telemedicine share equal footing. The appropriate setting depends on the person, the visit and current availability.

Office setting

Office primary care

Primary-care visits shaped around prevention, medication review and ongoing health needs, arranged by appointment.

  • Annual wellness visits
  • Preventive screenings
  • Medication review and management
  • Health education
  • Chronic condition follow-up
Home or facility

Home & facility visits

Primary care in a home or residential setting when the requested visit, location and clinical needs are appropriate.

  • Primary care visits at home
  • Fall-risk and safety assessment
  • Post-hospital follow-up
  • Caregiver and family coordination
  • Support for mobility limitations
Virtual setting

Telemedicine

Virtual visits across Maryland and Washington, DC, for eligible concerns when an in-person examination is not required.

  • Follow-up visits
  • Medication refills and reviews
  • Lab-result discussions
  • Chronic disease check-ins
  • Care planning
Connected care

Care coordination

Communication and planning for patients and families managing several moving parts.

  • Extended care planning
  • Medication coordination
  • Family and caregiver communication
  • Preventive health planning
  • Coordination with specialists
Important: Available services depend on clinical appropriateness, location, scheduling and coverage. Call the office to confirm whether a service is available for you.

Why the setting matters

The visit setting is part of the care plan.

An office can support a focused examination. A home or facility visit can reveal practical context. Telemedicine can make an appropriate follow-up easier. Nabia confirms the setting before scheduling.
